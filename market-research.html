FLAGSHIP RESEARCH PROJECT

AI-Assisted Market Decision Research Platform

A long-running independent engineering and research program exploring AI-assisted experimentation, Python simulation, sequential decision systems, and rigorous evaluation using real-world market data.

The specific market instruments, production decision logic, parameters, and proprietary research findings have intentionally been abstracted. This case study focuses on the engineering, experimentation, and evaluation systems developed around the research.

The Challenge

Researching decision systems in noisy real-world environments creates several engineering challenges. Strong apparent results can be caused by overfitting, data leakage, favorable outliers, inconsistent execution assumptions, or accidental changes in experimental methodology.

I developed a structured research environment designed to turn ambiguous ideas into reproducible experiments while maintaining strict separation between discovery and evaluation.

SYSTEM DESIGN

Research Architecture

I designed the research environment as a modular pipeline so that data preparation, experimentation, execution, evaluation, and reporting could evolve independently while remaining reproducible.

Market Data Historical and synthetic research inputs
↓
Context & Data Pipeline Validation, transformation, and experiment-ready inputs
↓
AI-Assisted Research Layer Hypothesis development, implementation, debugging, and analysis
↓
Decision & Simulation Engine Controlled execution of candidate policies and research rules
↓
Evaluation Framework Isolated validation, stress testing, and robustness analysis
↓
Research Release Frozen configurations, reports, documentation, and reproducible results

What I Built

Experiment Engine

Python tooling for defining, executing, comparing, and reproducing controlled research experiments.

AI-Assisted Workflow

Human-directed LLM workflows for hypothesis generation, implementation, debugging, analysis, and documentation.

Simulation Framework

Deterministic replay infrastructure for testing decision policies under timing, cost, resource, and outcome constraints.

Evaluation Pipeline

Isolated discovery, validation, and confirmation environments designed to prevent evaluation data from influencing development.

Robustness Testing

Stress tests designed to detect strategies whose apparent performance depends excessively on rare favorable outcomes.

Research Governance

Versioned rules, experiment freezes, documentation, validation criteria, and reproducible research releases.

Research Lifecycle

Research Question → Hypothesis → Experiment → Discovery → Freeze → Validation → Stress Test → Confirmation

Engineering Skills Demonstrated

Python · LLM Workflows · Data Analysis · Simulation · Experiment Design · Time-Series Analysis · Validation · Synthetic Data · Research Automation · Git · Reproducibility · Technical Documentation

Key Lessons

The project increasingly shifted from simply searching for strong results toward designing systems capable of distinguishing robust behavior from misleading experimental success.

This required treating evaluation methodology, reproducibility, and information isolation as core engineering problems rather than secondary analysis tasks.
